All prices are quoted in Ghanaian Cedis (GH¢). Final pricing depends on the materials, print colours, quantity, and any applicable fees, which may include a design fee, a die-cut board fee, and (for genuinely custom shapes) a custom die-cutting fee. Custom die-cutting fees are waived on repeat orders of the same design. Orders of 1,000 units or more receive a 5% discount on production cost (this discount does not apply to delivery charges). Kraapht Bags Ltd reserves the right to adjust these fees and discount terms at its discretion. Kraapht Bags Ltd is not currently VAT-registered, and VAT is not charged on orders; this section will be updated if that changes.

4.4 Payment

All payments are processed exclusively through Paystack (card and mobile money). Kraapht Bags Ltd does not accept cash on collection or any payment method outside Paystack. A deposit of 50% of the total order value is required at checkout to confirm the order. The remaining balance must be paid in full before the order is dispatched.

4.5 Production Timeline

Production begins one business day after your deposit is received. Payments made on a Saturday will have production begin the following Monday. Estimated production turnaround for your order is provided on the website at the time of ordering.

4.6 Outstanding Balances

If the outstanding balance remains unpaid, Kraapht Bags Ltd will send a written reminder at least 30 days before the three-month deadline, giving you a final opportunity to pay. If the balance remains unpaid three months from the scheduled dispatch date, Kraapht Bags Ltd may dispose of the materials and completed goods, and the deposit paid will not be refunded.

4.7 Delivery

For deliveries within Accra, a flat delivery fee of GH¢30 applies per order. For deliveries outside Accra, Kraapht Bags Ltd will typically arrange delivery through a third-party transport provider; the applicable delivery charge depends on the transport option available and is communicated to the customer in advance. In these cases, the customer pays the delivery charge directly to the third-party provider.
